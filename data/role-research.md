# What each starting role needs

Research behind the "Or start from a role" menu on the Resume page. I read real postings and role guides for each role, scored each area from 0 to 3 in each source, averaged the scores, and mapped the average to a slider level (2.6 and up Critical, 1.5 and up Important, 0.5 and up Nice to have, otherwise Not needed). The five security disciplines (vulnerability, identity, architecture, detection, cloud) are never lower than Nice to have, because a posting that does not mention one is not saying nobody needs it. The scoring script is `role-research.js`, so the numbers can be re-run or adjusted.

The scores are my reading of each source, not counts, so treat a result near a boundary as a judgment call. Postings written by a vendor lean toward that vendor's topic (the two Wiz articles lean toward cloud).
